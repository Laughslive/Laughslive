Privacy

Short version: LaughsLive has no accounts, sets no cookies and doesn't collect your name, email or precise location. Here's exactly what happens when you use the site.

Last updated November 7, 2026.

Your location

When you type a neighborhood, town or zip code, your browser matches it to a map point and works out distances itself. What you typed is saved only in your own browser (so it's remembered next time) and is never sent to us. LaughsLive doesn't ask for your device's GPS location.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. It records aggregate information such as which pages were viewed, the referring site, the browser and device type, and the country, so we can see which cities people use. We can't identify individual visitors from it.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the lettering comes from Google Fonts. Like any web host or font service, they receive your IP address when your browser loads the page, under their own privacy policies (GitHub's and Google's). We don't receive or store those logs.

Things stored on your device

  • The last location you searched, so you don't have to retype it.
  • Whether you dismissed the “add to home screen” tip.
  • A copy of pages you've viewed, so the app can open without a signal. Clearing your browser's data for laughslive.com removes all of this.

Links to other sites

Ticket buttons take you to venues and ticket sellers such as Ticketmaster. Once you're there, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. We don't add you to any list unless you sign up for one yourself.

Children

LaughsLive is a general-audience site and isn't directed at children under 13. Many listed shows are 18+ or 21+.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: info@laughslive.com.
