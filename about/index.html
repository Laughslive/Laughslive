About LaughsLive

LaughsLive is a free guide to live comedy: stand-up, improv and live podcast tapings in 29 U.S. cities and their suburbs, all in one list you can sort by night, distance and comedian.

Where the listings come from

  • Comedy clubs' own calendars. We read venues' own websites — Zanies, the Funny Bones, Comedy Works, Helium, the Improvs, Cap City, the Comedy Mothership and many more — twice a week.
  • Comedians' official tour pages, for the comics and live podcasts we follow.
  • Ticketmaster, every morning, for theater, arena and club shows sold there.

Every “Tickets” button goes to the venue's own page or its official ticket seller — never a resale site. Prices shown are the venue's own where listed. We don't sell tickets and aren't paid per click.

Missing a show? Something wrong?

Comedians, venues and fans: if a show is missing, a time has changed, or something sold out, email shows@laughslive.com with the comedian, venue, city, date and time, and a ticket link. We check submissions against the venue's own listing before adding them.

Contact

General questions, partnerships and advertising: info@laughslive.com.

Get the app

LaughsLive works like an app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap Get the app at the top of any page.
