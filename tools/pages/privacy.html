Privacy

Short version: LaughsLive has no accounts and doesn't collect your name or precise location. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies, and Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. We only get your email address if you sign up for the newsletter. Here's exactly what happens when you use the site.

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Your location

When you type a neighborhood, town or zip code, your browser matches it to a map point and works out distances itself. What you typed is saved only in your own browser (so it's remembered next time) and is never sent to us. LaughsLive doesn't ask for your device's GPS location.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. It records aggregate information such as which pages were viewed, the referring site, the browser and device type, and the country, so we can see which cities people use. We can't identify individual visitors from it.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It sets cookies (such as _ga) and collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search engine or social media), your browser and device type, and your approximate location (city or region, worked out from your IP address). We also count clicks on ticket buttons, the “Show more” button and newsletter sign-ups (only that one happened and which city was picked, never your email address). We never send Google what you type into the location or comedian search.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy and may use it as described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracking settings, a content blocker, or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. We see only totals and trends, not who you are.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the lettering comes from Google Fonts. Like any web host or font service, they receive your IP address when your browser loads the page, under their own privacy policies (GitHub's and Google's). We don't receive or store those logs.

Things stored on your device

  • Google Analytics cookies (see above).
  • The last location you searched, so you don't have to retype it.
  • Whether you dismissed the “add to home screen” tip.
  • A copy of pages you've viewed, so the app can open without a signal. Clearing your browser's data for laughslive.com removes all of this.

Links to other sites

Ticket buttons take you to venues and ticket sellers such as Ticketmaster. Once you're there, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the weekly email, we store only your email address and the city you picked, so we can send you that city's shows. Sign-ups are handled by Brevo, our email provider, which keeps this list for us under its own privacy policy and sends the emails. Brevo's code isn't loaded on our pages; your email and city are sent to Brevo only when you press Sign up. You'll get one email to confirm before you're added, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell or share the list. To be removed or to have your details deleted, use the unsubscribe link or email info@laughslive.com.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. We don't add you to any list unless you sign up for one yourself.

Children

LaughsLive is a general-audience site and isn't directed at children under 13. Many listed shows are 18+ or 21+.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: info@laughslive.com.
